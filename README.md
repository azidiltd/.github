# Azidi

Azidi ([azidi.co.ke](https://azidi.co.ke)) is a Kenya-based technology company focused on helping organizations solve real business problems through practical digital solutions.

## What Azidi does

Azidi supports clients across the full product lifecycle, from idea to delivery and improvement:

- **Software development** for web and mobile platforms
- **Business systems implementation** and process digitization
- **Cloud and infrastructure support** for scalable, reliable operations
- **Technology consulting** to align tools with business goals
- **Maintenance and continuous improvement** for existing systems

## How Azidi works

Azidi emphasizes:

- **Problem-first delivery**: building solutions around measurable outcomes
- **Partnership**: working closely with client teams, not just handing over code
- **Practical innovation**: choosing technology that is sustainable and fit for purpose
- **Long-term value**: creating systems that are maintainable and ready to grow

## Industries and impact

Azidi works with businesses and institutions that need dependable digital systems to improve efficiency, service delivery, and decision-making.
